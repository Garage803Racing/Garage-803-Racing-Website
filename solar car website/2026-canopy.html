Finishing the Canopy

07/07/2026

Since April and May 2026, we worked on using XPS foam to shape the canopy for the car. During the summer, we used a heat gun to bend acrylic sheets, a transparent material used for the windshield. Using the foam mold as a guide, the windshield was formed while part of the foam mold for the canopy was cut off for the windshield. While Yuriy Korniychuk worked on assembling the canopy, Wilder White and Jerry Liang worked on creating steel parts to create the system that allows the canopy to open outwards.
