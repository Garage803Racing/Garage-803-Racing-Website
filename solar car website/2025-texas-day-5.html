We Passed Scrutineering!

July 19th, 2025

Today was our last day of scrutineering, which meant we had to make a big push to finish the last four stations. We headed back to the electrical station, where we got more criteria checked off, but there were still a couple issues so we decided to fix that later in the day. We went to the tilt and turn station, where our car must tolerate a 20 degree tilt sideways without any part collapsing, and the car must be able to make a U-turn within 15 meters. Everyone held their breath in the tilting part, but it was a huge sigh of relief after passing that section. We then knocked out the slalom station with a more balanced car, as well as the safety vehicle station, where we practiced loading our car onto a trailer in case of an emergency.

By lunch time, we only had the electrical station left to go, before needing to pass the final inspection, basically the inspection of all inspections. We worked on fixing our high voltage, where we did some rewiring while ensuring the solar panels could charge the battery. In our final hours of scrutineering, we faced some problems such as the solar panels not charging, but thanks to a judge and the Seattle Solar Car team who supplied us some parts, we were able to get that fixed.

It was around 7pm, and the hard deadline for scrutineering is at 8pm, no extensions. We brought our car to the electrical station, and finally got our last two missing criteria checked off, before pulling our car to the final inspection, where a group of judges do a last check on our car to make sure the car is safe and drivable. We all held our breath as the the judges scanned every inch of our car. Many moments later, the head judge took a deep breath, saying how we are so close to the deadline, we met all the criteria, and there are only super minor fixes left to make, but despite that, he eventually uncapped his pen, and made the final signature on our scrutineering book.

At long last, after spending our entire summer so far on building our car, we have made it to this moment, where we passed scrutineering and qualified the race at 7:32pm, only 28 minutes before the hard deadline. We all set down whatever tool we had in our hands and took a moment to celebrate, before getting ready to finally race the next day.
