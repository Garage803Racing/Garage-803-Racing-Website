MAKO

Year: 2026

Division: Advanced

Ranking*: Day One 4th Place

Distance Driven**: 114.3 km

Top Speed: 95 km/h


Mako was our second solar car made by our team and our first ever Advanced Division solar car. Like last year, we decided to continue the shark face on the aeroshell, giving our car the name "Mako" which means shark. From the support of our sponsors, we were able to join the Advanced Division as we were generously donated with high quality parts such as higher efficiency solar cells and high voltage battery.

DNF

Year: 2025

Division: Classic

Ranking*: 3rd Place

Distance Driven**: 881.0 km

Top Speed: 65 km/h


DNF was the first solar car we made, and was competed in the Classic Division. Despite its name standing for "Did Not Finish," the ironic part was we indeed did finish the car and qualified for the race. It is called DNF because we forgot to submit an actual name for our car, and we decided to just keep its name this way. This car was known for chasing a clownfish looking solar car from another team on the Texas Motor Speedway.
