Solar Array

07/07/2026

For the solar array, while we originally wanted to use our shaped foam as the base for it, we realized it would not be a strong support for our solar cells especially when tilting it at an angle. Like the rest of the car, we made a structural frame of the car using aluminum, and bent some of the aluminum pieces to create that curvature. Then, we cut out polycarbonate sheets to put on top of the array frame to serve as a platform to hold the solar cells. When attaching the solar panels, we used a small electric spinning blade to cut out channels on the polycarbonate to fit in the wires, while our teammates Aadi Joseph and Lezhi Zeng worked on soldering the wires. Lastly, we used VHB tape to secure the solar cells tightly onto the array.
