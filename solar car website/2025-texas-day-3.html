First Day of Scrutineering

July 17th, 2025

Today was our third day in Texas and our first day of scrutineering. After a whole night of working, we trailered our car to the Texas Motor Speedway. Even though we were extremely tired, we were impressed to set foot in a racecourse for the first time, as we were amazed by large racetrack, the giant billboard sign, and the stadium. Upon arriving, we unloaded our car, with the help of the other team, into the garage and then lined up to sign up for the scrutineering stations.

Basically, how scrutineering works is that there are a total of 6 inspection stations we need to pass: Mechanical, Electrical, Tilt & Turn, Brake Test, Safety Vehicle, and Slalom. Today we got the mechanical and electrical elements of our car inspected. The judges told us most of the criteria have been met, but there were still some fixing to do, so we have to check in again with them later. We spent most of the day continuing to secure our mechanical systems, making sure the bolts were tight enough and no loose wires. However, because of the all-nighter we pulled, most of us were exhausted, as Liam, Yuriy, and Jerry fell asleep during the middle of our car getting inspected.

After a day of scrutineering, we attended the solar car opening ceremony, where we enjoyed a meal while socializing with the other solar car teams, before heading back to our house. Because the scrutineering stations and the garage closes in the early night at 9pm each day, we were able to get enough sleep today because we couldn't work on the car until the garage opened the next day at 7am.
