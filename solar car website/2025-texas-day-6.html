First Day of Racing

July 20th, 2025

Today was our first day getting to race our car on the Texas Motor Speedway. We showed up in the morning right when the garage opened to bring our car out under the sun to charge the batteries, before making our last fixes to the wiring that the judge mentioned the day before. Since we drew Number 1 on team check in day, we were actually the first car to enter the racetrack before everyone else. Our car actually held up pretty well, and we managed to be in the top 3 of the leaderboard so far.

During our mandatory 2 hour lunch break (no one can race during that time), we set out our car under the sun to recharge the car's battery before setting back out again. When the race day ended at 5pm, all teams drove their car back to the garage area and set their car under the sun again to charge. So far, our car drove a total of 97 laps (equivalent to 145.5 miles) and ranked 3rd place in the Classic Division.
