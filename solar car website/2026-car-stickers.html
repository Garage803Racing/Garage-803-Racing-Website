Car Stickers & Livery

07/08/2026

While the rest of our team was working on the mechanical systems of the car, Lukas Abelson, Angela Liu, and Jerry Liang worked on decorating the aeroshell. This process started off with sanding the aeroshell to ensure a smooth surface, then followed by spraying 3 coats of epoxy primer over the aeroshell. After that, Angela Liu went to get some 2K Single Stage Aerosol white and dark blue paint to add color to the aeroshell. During that process, Jerry Liang reached out to some sticker companies that would help print out logos of the sponsors that supported us. Luckily, SpeedPro of SF Peninsula Graphics company was willing to support our team with a ~70% discount to create the stickers, earning their logo to be put on our car thanks to their support. Once the stickers arrived, we carefully placed them on the aeroshell of our car.
