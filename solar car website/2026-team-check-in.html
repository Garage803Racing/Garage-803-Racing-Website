2026 Team Check-In

07/15/2026

Today was the team-check in day for the 2026 Texas Solar Car Competition. We spent the morning doing some more low voltage wiring. As the afternoon approached, we went to the Marriot Hotel near the Texas Motor Speedway to meet other solar car teams, get our scrutineering handbook, and submit other materials like our data sheets for our car parts. Then, the drivers and the safety officer of our team went to attend the safety and drivers info meeting before we headed back to continue finishing our car throughout the night.
