Class D Fire Extinguisher

04/30/2026

Today, thanks to Ace Fire Inc, we were able to get borrow and get our hands on a Class D Fire Extinguisher, a crucial component of the open road race across Texas. Ace Fire Inc. is a company in the San Francisco Bay Area that provides fire prevention equipment and training. The Class D fire extinguisher that they generously rented to us will help prevent or put out any accidental metal fires that could, although unlikely, occur. Additionally, Ace Fire Inc provided our team with a donation to help fund our trip to Texas. We are extremely grateful to partner with Ace Fire Inc. and we hope to work together again in the future.
