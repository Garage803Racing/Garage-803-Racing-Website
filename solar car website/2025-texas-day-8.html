Third Day of Racing

July 22nd, 2025

Today was our third day of racing. As usual, our car continued to drive lap after lap, passing cars while we all watched on the side. Something that was funny was that there was another solar car team where their car was shaped like Nemo (clownfish), while ours was shaped like a shark, and so that team really wanted our car to go behind their's to make it look like a shark was chasing them. When our cars were next to each other, their car even slowed down, hoping that one of us would be able to get a photo.

Anyways, after racing for a whole morning, we enjoyed a nice pasta lunch. After eating, we realized our low voltage battery (not powered by our solar panels unlike our high voltage battery) was critically low, setting off the alarm in our car. Our team captain set out to buy another battery while the rest of us asked team after team if they had a spare low voltage battery we could use, and miraculously, one of them did! We borrowed their low voltage battery while they helped charge our's so that we could continue racing. Despite the 1 hour time loss, we were still able to make up most of the laps, allowing us to continue securing 3rd place in the leaderboard overall.
