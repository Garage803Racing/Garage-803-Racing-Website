Garage 803 Racing relies on the generosity of sponsors and donors. Their support gives students the opportunity to apply their engineering skills in a real world project while learning about sustainability in engineering. Help support the next generation of engineers in bringing our solar car to life.

WHAT WE DO

We will design and build a one-seater solar car that's fully dependent on solar energy. This car will be designed to race on the Texas Motor Speedway over the course of 4 days during the summer (2025 event). Throughout the season, we will research, design, and manufacture a race-capable solar car for the event in Dallas.

WHY WE NEED YOUR HELP

We need YOUR help to support us financially as we compete in the High School Solar Car Challenge in Texas! Building and racing a solar-powered car requires funding for materials, tools, travel, and competition fees. Your donation will enable us to bring this project to life, allowing students to gain hands-on STEM experience while advancing sustainable technology and awareness. Together, we can drive innovation and inspire future problem-solvers!
