Classic Division 3rd Place!

July 23rd, 2025

At long last, from cutting our first ever piece of metal and learning how to use power tools to scoring 3rd place from racing a car we built from the ground up, participating in the Texas Solar Car Competition was such an unforgettable experience, and it is impressive to see how far we (and all the other teams, of course), have come. Thank you to all the friends, family, and other solar car teams (especially Seattle Solar Car), for supporting us through each step of our process, from planning, to building, all the way to racing, especially since we were a rookie team. It was because of you that we were able to build the car, and overcome all the crashouts that we had along the way. To all the solar car teams out there, we hope to see you again in the 2026 season so that we can continue our journey in the challenge and help all team members get the hands-on experience to successfully build and compete!
