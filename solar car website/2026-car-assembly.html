Assembling the Car

07/11/2026

Today, we finished up all the vital functionality of the car, like the steering and electrical system in order to be able to drive the car around smoothly. After that, some of us first took the car (without the solar array) to an open parking lot to test its performance, while the rest of our team stayed back to finish assembling the solar cells onto the array. Our car had a testing speed of ~35 miles per hour with no major issues in steering and balance. After the rest of the team finished with the solar array, we attached the solar array onto the car through inserting bolts through the array's swingarm.

During the whole construction process, many parents worked on helping to build a wooden enclosure on the trailer to ensure our car would be protected during transport. They carefully planned to make sure the car's size was accounted for before using wooden planks and plywood to build the enclosure. After assembling the car, we were able to safely pack the solar car into the trailer to transport to Texas.
