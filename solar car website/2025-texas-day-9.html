Final Day of Racing

July 23rd, 2025

Today is the day, the final day of racing. After charging our car under the sun as much as possible, we continued racing. Since we didn't need to worry about saving battery anymore as it was our last day, we raced a lot faster, driving speeds up to 35-40 miles per hour. We enjoyed a nice meal of burgers for lunch, before pushing in our final laps of the 2025 Texas Solar Car Competition. At the end of the final race day, took our final group photos in front of our car, before cleaning up and loading everything back on the trailer.

After a couple hours of chilling, we attended the closing ceremony, where we had dinner with all the solar car teams, then all the team captains of all the solar car teams gave a speech, before the director Lehman Marks read out the team rankings. After 4 days of racing, we were able to score 3rd place in the Classic Division, with a total of 382 laps or 573 miles driven. When the ceremony ended, we socialized with the other teams, before saying goodbye, and spending another full night packing up to go back home, wrapping up our journey in the 2025 Texas Solar Car Challenge.
