Cardboard Safety Frame Replica

03/08/2026

The process of building our new car for the 2026 Texas Solar Car Challenge has started. Before building the actual frame with aluminum, we decided to make a 1:1 replica of our safety frame using cardboard. This was to ensure that all the components such as the batteries, mechanical systems, and more importantly, that the driver would fit. We went on to also make a replica of the aeroshell to help guide our process of building later.
