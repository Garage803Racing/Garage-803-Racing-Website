Foam Canopy Mold

04/21/2026

The process of making the foam canopy (Driver's window) is similar to the aeroshell, except this time it is just the mold. Like the aeroshell, we curved the foam by making straight shallow cuts down the foam, and used wooden dowels, super glue, and spray foam to stick the components together. Then, we used a sander to cut down any excess dried glue and spray foam and round the edges. Our plan is to use heat to bend acrylic (clear, thick sheet of plastic) and use our foam canopy as a mold or reference to help us make the transparent canopy (so the driver can see!).
