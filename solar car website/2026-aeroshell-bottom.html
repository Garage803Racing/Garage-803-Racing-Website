Creating the Aeroshell

04/16/2026

This year's aeroshell, we aim to have a thicker aeroshell, and give it a more sleek, aerodynamic shape. Thanks to Lowes and Home Depot, we got several large sheets of 1 inch thick XPS foam boards. XPS foam is toxic so we cannot use heat to bend it, so what we did was we made shallow cuts at the bending points. We went along with the cardboard mockup of the aeroshell to make more precise shapes in respect with our CAD model.

To attach the foam components together, we used wooden dowels and stick them into the foam (similar to screws holding two pieces of wood together), and then used glue and foam spray to fill in the gaps. While the glue was drying, we used masking tape to hold the foam pieces together. After the glue dried, we used a sander to smoothen the edges (make the edges more round), and cut off any excess glue.
