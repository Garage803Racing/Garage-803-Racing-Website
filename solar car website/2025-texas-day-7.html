Second Day of Racing

July 21st, 2025

Today was our second day of racing. Like day one, we followed the same process of showing up early to charge our car under the sun. Our car was able to hold up as usual, driving lap after lap on the racetrack. One major event that happened on the track was another team's car broke down, forcing everyone to stop and patiently wait until everything was clear. When it was noon, we had our daily two hour lunch break, before continuing to race. Our car continued to run without trouble, even overtaking a few cars. At the end of the day, we drove a total of 93 more laps, totaling in 140 laps (We got some lap deduction penalties from accidentally violating small safety rules like not wearing a hat, putting us at 135 laps), still putting us in a decently strong position on the leaderboard.
