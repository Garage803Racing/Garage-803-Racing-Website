Scrutineering Day 2

July 18th, 2025

Today was our second day of scrutineering. We made our finishing touches to our mechanical system, ensuring all the parts were intact, before finally completing that station and getting it signed by a judge. Then, we transitioned to making adjustments to our electrical system, until testing our car and realizing our motor was not working. Luckily, we had another solar car team help us, eventually spotting a wiring mistake and helping us fix it, allowing our motor to work again.

Fixing our motor meant we could continue with scrutineering, doing all the stations where our car must be driven. We decided to do the braking test, making sure the car would be able to quickly stop after driving above 20 miles per hour. After a few tries, we eventually passed that station, which meant there were only four stations left to go. To wrap up today, we decided to attempt the slalom station, where the car has to be driven in a rounded zig-zag pattern along some traffic cones. Our car seemed to tilt violently, and only one out of our three drivers passed. We headed back to our garage, ultimately deciding to switch our car's supensions with solid aluminum bars. Even though driving the car would feel less smooth on bumpy terrain, it was able to maintain balance much easier, making us more prepared for the scrutineering stations the next day.
