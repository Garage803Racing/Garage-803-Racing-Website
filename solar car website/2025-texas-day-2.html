Team Check-In

July 16th, 2025

Today was our second day in Texas. After having breakfast, we went to the Marriot Hotel near the Texas Motor Speedway to check in for the competition, get our scrutineering handbook, meet other teams, and most importantly the event organizer we love, Lehman Marks. After the meetup, we headed back to enjoy a nice meal before continuing to work on the car, going along the scrutineering handbook to make sure we fulfilled as much criteria as we can. During the night, we met some solar car teams who were across the street and checked out their cars. We worked into the night, reattaching the solar array, aeroshell, and make sure the car is in working condition, eventually pulling an all-nighter before entering scrutineering the next day.
