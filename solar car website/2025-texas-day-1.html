First Day in Texas

July 15th, 2025

Today was our first full day in Texas (we landed yesterday). Ryan arrived with the solar car earlier than expected, so after a quick breakfast, we got to work. We spent a lot of time fixing and reinforcing the different parts of the car as some parts fell off during transport, while other parts were weak. After hours of work, we enjoyed a Texas BBQ lunch, and then worked on finalizing paperwork and schematics. During the evening, we continued to work on the car, making sure the frames, the steering system, motor mounts, and solar charge controllers are all secured in place.

After a day of work, we treated ourselves with some games of pool, and recording some funny Instagram videos for other solar car teams to see. We are getting so close to entering the competition and we look forward to meeting the other solar car teams the next day.
